### Our Vision
Our vision is to foster a society where every citizen, utilizing the rights enshrined in the Constitution, evolves into a capable and responsible individual, contributing to the nation’s development.

### Our Mission
At Vasundhara Welfare Foundation, our mission is to empower every child with special needs by unlocking their full potential and guiding them towards a life of dignity and self-reliance.

## About Us

Vasundhara Welfare Foundation, a non-profit organization, was established on 04 November 2015 under the Madhya Pradesh Societies Registration Act, 1973. The foundation was born out of the inspiration and guidance of the former Union Environment Minister, Late Shri Anil Madhav Dave, whose vision for a better India continues to inspire our work. Since its inception, the foundation has been committed to continuous progress, driven by a mission to uplift communities and nurture the environment through various service-oriented initiatives.

### Our organization is driven by the following core objectives:

- To ensure availability of equal health, education, education and other basic facilities to all persons with disabilities to every section of the society.
- To provide access to various welfare schemes run by the government to connect the deprived sections of the society with the mainstream.
- To operate and manage projects related to environmental protection, sanitation, safety and quality, sustainable and profitable and organic agriculture, biodiversity and animal welfare.
- To motivate every citizen of the country to discharge their civic duties while strengthening social, religious and cultural unity in the nation.

## Our Services for Children with Special Needs

Vasundhara Welfare Foundation is dedicated to providing comprehensive support to children with special needs. Our services are designed to nurture their abilities and help them achieve their highest potential. We focus on the following areas:

### . Special Education
Our special education programs cater to the unique learning needs of children with disabilities, providing tailored support to help them overcome challenges and succeed academically.

### . Sports and Cultural Activities
We organize sports and cultural activities to enhance physical fitness, social interaction, and cultural awareness among children with disabilities, helping build their confidence and self-esteem.
