About MPK Samiti

मातृ-पितृ कृपा शैक्षिक स्वास्थ्य पर्यावरण खेल संबंध सामाजिक विकास और कल्याण समिति

Established on 24 August 2022, MPK Samiti is a dedicated non-profit organization driven by a deep commitment to uplift communities and nurture the environment. We work tirelessly to provide service-oriented initiatives.

From empowering women and children to offering a safe haven for seniors and those battling addiction, we believe in the power of collective effort to build a society that embraces diversity and ensures a fulfilling life for everyone.

Our Core Objectives

  • Ensure equal access to quality education, specialized training, and healthcare facilities.
  • Operate comprehensive rehabilitation programs and old age homes.
  • Promote environmental protection and sustainable practices.
  • Empower women and youth through skill development and sports.

Our Vision

Our vision is to foster a compassionate and empowered society where every individual—from children with special needs to senior citizens—receives equal opportunities, care, and dignity to contribute to the nation's overall development.

Our Mission

At MPK Samiti, our mission is to provide comprehensive support through specialized education, reliable senior care, drug rehabilitation, women empowerment, and environmental conservation, ensuring holistic development for all marginalized sections of society.

Our Key Programs

Special School

श्री विद्याकुंज स्पेशल स्कूल

Our special education programs cater to the unique learning needs of children with disabilities, providing tailored support to help them overcome challenges, build confidence, and succeed academically.

Senior Care

सशुल्क एवं निःशुल्क वरिष्ठ जन आवास गृह

Providing a safe, respectful, and caring residential environment for senior citizens, ensuring their medical, emotional, and everyday needs are met with compassion and dignity.

De-addiction Center

नशाबंदी एवं नशामुक्ति प्रचार प्रसार केन्द्र

Dedicated to guiding individuals towards a substance-free life through comprehensive rehabilitation, counseling, and lasting emotional support.

Sports Academy

श्री विद्याकुंज खेल एकेडमी

Organizing sports and physical activities to enhance fitness, teamwork, and social interaction among youth and children, helping build their confidence and discipline.

Women Empowerment

बाल विकास एवं महिला सशक्तिकरण केंद्र

Empowering women and children through skill development, rights awareness, and personal growth opportunities to ensure self-reliance and independence.

Environment Conservation

पर्यावरण संरक्षण केन्द्र

Leading community efforts in environmental protection, sustainable practices, and nature conservation to create a healthier ecosystem for future generations.
