### Website Structure & Layout Plan (Inspired by vasundharawelfare.org)

**1. Navigation Bar & Header:**
- Logo: MPK Samiti (मातृ-पितृ कृपा शैक्षिक स्वास्थ्य पर्यावरण खेल संबंध सामाजिक विकास और कल्याण समिति बांदा)
- Links: Home | About Us | Branches & Programs | Resources & Reports | Gallery | Contact Us
- Call to Action: "Donate Us" Button

**2. Main Page Sections:**
- **Hero Section:** High-quality image showing social work, with a compelling headline (Vision/Mission).
- **About Us Summary:** Brief history and registration info (NPO certificate, etc.). 
- **Our Key Branches / Works (Organizational Programs):**
  * *School & Education:* Special School (श्री विद्याकुंज स्पेशल स्कूल) / Education & Training Center
  * *Social & Senior Care:* Old Age Home (सशुल्क एवं निःशुल्क वरिष्ठ जन आवास गृह)
  * *De-addiction:* Drug De-addiction and Rehabilitation Center (नशाबंदी एवं नशामुक्ति प्रचार प्रसार केन्द्र)
  * *Sports & Development:* Sports Academy (श्री विद्याकुंज खेल एकेडमी)
  * *Women & Child Development:* Mahila Shashaktikaran Kendra (बाल विकास एवं महिला सशक्तिकरण केंद्र)
  * *Environment:* Environment Conservation Center (पर्यावरण संरक्षण केन्द्र)
- **Impact & Reach:** Statistics section (e.g., Number of people helped, programs organized).
- **Social Work & Community:** Section highlighting help provided to poor and needy.

**3. Resources & Governance (Internal Governance):**
- Dedicated page or section to host PDF documents (Audit Reports, ITR, Registration Certificates, NPO Darpan, Niyamawali).
